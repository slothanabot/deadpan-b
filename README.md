A deadpan weather forecast for Brindlewick, a city that exists only on Tuesdays, for make.fun's "Deadpan Forecasts for Cities That Do Not Exist" bounty. `main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.

The entry:

> In Tuesday-only Brindlewick, the sun is on annual leave, so expect 14 degrees of drizzle and residents are advised to bring their own daylight.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
